\section{Introduction}
According to~\cite{Tavares.}, software synthesis is a process where a customized code is automatically generated from system-level specification. Today, there is always a push for market growth in the segment of Silicon industry. The demand for high performance, low power consumption, and cost-effective solutions is increasing. In quest of embedding many-processor systems on a single chip (MPSoCs), system complexities are growing at an almost exponential rate~\cite{Gajski.2009}. The demand for sophisticated high-performance, low-powered processors to meet the system complexities is unprecedented for today's applications. Howerver, the traditional design processes, ``in which systems are designed directly at the low hardware or software levels''~\cite{Gajski.2009}, are fast becoming obsolete and infeasible to bridge the gap ``generated by the disparity between the rapid paces at which design complexity has increased in comparison to that of design productivity''~\cite{Gajski.2009}. In this context, the software-synthesis approach offers a viable design solution.\\
Most major semiconductor roadmaps suggest a raise in the level of abstraction in the design process as an acceptable solution for closing the productivity gap~\cite{Gajski.2009}. Higher abstraction levels is more aligned with the system requirements and specifications. Software synthesis can bridge the semantic gap between higher abstraction levels and low-level implementation~\cite{Gajski.2009}. As stated in~\cite{Abdi.}, software must be developed specifically for the given cores, platform, applications and mapping for Hardware-dependent-Software (HdS). Also described by~\cite{Abdi.} that the manual HdS development can become tiem consuming not only due to the code size, but also due to the complex interaction processes in concurrent applications mapped to multi-core platforms. The software synthesis approach enables the automatic generation of code from high-level specifications, thus reducing the complexity and time required for manual coding. More specifically, this is a design methodology that allows designers to apply ``design-automation techniques for modeling, simulation, synthesis, and verification to the system-design process''~\cite{Gajski.2009}.
